NEPRA Utility Tariffs & Pakistan Tax Regulations

Clear, transparent explanations of every line item on Pakistani electricity bills.

Tariff Surcharge

What is Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA)?

FPA represents the variation in the actual cost of generation fuel (imported coal, RLNG, furnace oil, hydro) versus NEPRA's reference tariff. It is charged retrospectively with 1 to 2 months lag.

Tax Breakdown

Understanding GST & Electricity Duty

Standard Pakistani utility bills carry 18% General Sales Tax (GST) under the Sales Tax Act, plus 1.5% Provincial Electricity Duty (ED). Additional withholding tax (WHT) applies to non-filers on commercial meters.

Consumer Rights

How to Dispute Inaccurate Meter Readings

If your bill's present reading is ahead of your physical meter index (over-reading), photograph the meter index and file a complaint through the DISCO customer care portal or NEPRA Consumer Affairs.

Tariff Slabs

Protected vs Unprotected Consumer Rules

Protected consumers are defined as domestic consumers whose monthly consumption has consistently remained under 200 kWh for the last 6 months. Exceeding 200 units even once resets this eligibility.
